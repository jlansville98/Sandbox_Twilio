# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026

SMS Testing Sandbox ("we," "our," or "us") operates a personal software development and testing SMS messaging service. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect
We collect only the mobile phone number and name you voluntarily provide to us via direct verbal consent for the purpose of receiving test SMS notifications.

## How We Use Your Information
Your information is used strictly to send software development and platform testing SMS messages and to manage opt-in, opt-out (STOP), and assistance (HELP) requests.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Opt-Out and Support
You may opt out of receiving messages at any time by replying **STOP** to any message. For assistance, reply **HELP** or contact the administrator directly.
